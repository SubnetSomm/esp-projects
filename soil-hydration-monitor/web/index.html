LED · checking…

Single pulse every 5s: hotspot monitoring. Double pulse every 5s: joined monitoring. Triple pulse every 5s: water needed. One short flash each second: watering. Rapid flashing: joining Wi-Fi. Long flash every 2s: check device. Steady: release button to sleep. Off: sleeping (or unpowered).

Calibration & device details

The lower end is the point when your plant needs water, not completely dry soil. The upper end is settled soil after watering. Changes rescale displayed history and restart recommendation checks; raw values remain saved.
